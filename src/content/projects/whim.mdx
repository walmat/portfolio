Fragile is the company and Whim is the product. I was mostly in charge of everything frontend.

Whim rents products by the month from the brands that make them. A customer picks Whim on a
partner brand's own site, and for one monthly price we ship the product to their door, new or
like-new. From there they can swap it, upgrade it, have it replaced, or request a quote and keep
it.

## Portal

my.whim.com is where customers manage what they have out. Four items in the sidebar—Home, Shop,
Payments, and Account—and Home opens on active orders, with completed cancellations behind a
disclosure.

An order opens under the brand it came from and lists the items in it. A modal holds the date
subscribed, the discount code, a link to the agreement, and the order number.

The shop is the newest part of the portal. Cards lead with a monthly rate and the retail price
struck through—$189/mo against $2,999 for an Eight Sleep Pod 5, $34/mo against $499 for a Reencle,
$99/mo against $1,795 for a Terra Kaffe TK-02. Above the grid is a bar with the three guarantees:
cancel anytime, free shipping, and no cost replacements.

<DesktopScreenshots screenshots={[
  { src: "/whim/product/portal-shop.webp", alt: "The shop inside the Whim portal" }

Product pages carry the image, the brand, the monthly rate against retail, a description, and a
Checkout button. Size variants are priced as monthly deltas. On the Pod 5, Queen is the default,
Full is -$20.00/mo, and King is +$10.00/mo.
