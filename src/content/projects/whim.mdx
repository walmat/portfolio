Whim is a rental subscription service for premium consumer products. Instead of buying a
projector, an espresso machine, or a sauna outright, you rent it by the month from the brand
that builds it, and you can swap it, upgrade it, or buy it whenever you want. Fragile is the
company behind it. I was responsible for the frontend.

That last sentence is the easy part to say and the hard part to prove, so the rest of this is
the specific work rather than the job title.
